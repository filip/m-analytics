Everything your dashboard shows, always one glance away

Two native apps built for their platforms, sharing one purpose: today's traffic, without the browser tab.

On the Mac

  • A menu bar number that's always there — visits today, visitors online, or logged-in members, your choice
  • A full popover or a dedicated window: hourly chart, today's summary, top pages, sources, countries, devices, a live visitor map, and expandable visit detail
  • Drag panels to rearrange the window exactly how you want it

On the iPhone

  • The same dashboard, redesigned for one scrolling screen
  • Home Screen widgets in three sizes — a quick who's-online glance, an hourly chart with today's headline numbers, and your latest visitors
  • Force a light or dark icon independent of system appearance

Built for real Matomo installs

  • Works with any Matomo instance — self-hosted or Matomo Cloud — using a read-only API token you create yourself
  • Handles real-world setups: plugins enabled after archives were built, servers on plain HTTP on a local network, tokens with limited access
  • Your server address and token stay on your device

Nothing passes through us

M-Analytics talks directly to the Matomo server you configure. No analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no ads, no servers of our own — there's nowhere for your data to go but where you sent it.
